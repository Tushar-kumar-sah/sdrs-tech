Our flagship product

OMNIGO

Move. Tow. Recover.

Connecting every tow. Powering every move.

OMNIGO is a technology platform designed to connect customers, towing companies, drivers and business operations through a modern digital experience.

It is a towing marketplace and dispatch technology platform: customers who need towing are connected with nearby towing providers and towing companies that onboard to the platform.

01 — Request

A customer requests towing assistance.

The journey starts with a simple request.

02 — Match

The platform connects the request with a suitable nearby provider.

OMNIGO is designed to bring customers and nearby towing providers together.

03 — Receive

The towing company or driver receives the job.

Towing companies onboard to the platform and manage the work they receive.

04 — Complete

The service progresses through dispatch and completion.

From dispatch to a completed job, each stage belongs to one connected system.
